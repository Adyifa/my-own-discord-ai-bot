# Events & Tournaments

## Weekly events
- **Valorant scrims:** every Saturday at 19:00 WIB in the "Scrim Room" voice channel. Teams are balanced by rank before the match.
- **Mabar Minecraft (play together):** every Sunday at 16:00 WIB on the community Minecraft server.

## Monthly MLBB tournament
- Held on the first Sunday of every month.
- Prize pool: Rp500.000 for the winning team and a special "Champion" role for one month.
- Teams of 5 players plus 1 optional substitute.
- Registration opens on Monday in #tournament-signup and closes on Friday at 23:59 WIB. Use the /register command there.

Late registrations are not accepted. Contact an Event Organizer if your team needs to swap a player.
