# Server Rules

Welcome to Nusantara Gaming Hub, a community for Indonesian gamers. Both Indonesian and English are fine everywhere.

## General
1. Be kind. Insults, harassment and hate speech lead to an immediate warning.
2. No NSFW content anywhere on the server.
3. Keep spoilers for new games, anime and films in the #spoilers channel and use spoiler tags (||like this||).
4. No advertising other Discord servers without permission from an admin.

## Trading
Selling or buying game accounts, joki (rank boosting) services and unofficial top-up resellers are not allowed here. The first offence is a warning; repeated offences lead to a ban.

## Warning policy
- 3 warnings: 1-day timeout.
- 5 warnings: permanent ban.
- Warnings expire after 90 days of good behaviour.

Friendly trash talk during matches is fine as long as it stays about the game and nobody asks you to stop.
